FONTLOG for the fonts in peachtree/

These fonts are metric-compatible with fonts shipped with Microsoft Office/Windows
(the same character widths), so documents keep their line and page breaks.
They contain no glyph outlines or other font data files from those fonts.

They are modified versions of these SIL Open Font License fonts:

- Gelasio: Copyright 2022 The Gelasio Project Authors (https://github.com/SorkinType/Gelasio)
- Noto Serif: Copyright 2022 The Noto Project Authors (https://github.com/notofonts/latin-greek-cyrillic)
- NotoSansMath: Copyright 2022 The Noto Project Authors (https://github.com/notofonts)
- NotoSansArmenian: Copyright 2022 The Noto Project Authors (https://github.com/notofonts)
- NotoSansArabic: Copyright 2022 The Noto Project Authors (https://github.com/notofonts)
- NotoSansBengali: Copyright 2022 The Noto Project Authors (https://github.com/notofonts)
- NotoSansGujarati: Copyright 2022 The Noto Project Authors (https://github.com/notofonts)
- NotoSansTamil: Copyright 2022 The Noto Project Authors (https://github.com/notofonts)
- NotoSansKhmer: Copyright 2022 The Noto Project Authors (https://github.com/notofonts)
- NotoSansMono: Copyright 2022 The Noto Project Authors (https://github.com/notofonts)

Styles: 14. Version 1.000.
Reserved Font Names of the base fonts are not used in these fonts' names.
